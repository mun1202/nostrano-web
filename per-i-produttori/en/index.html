How it works

  1. 1

    We get to know each other

    You leave the name of your farm and your WhatsApp number. We call you and set up the listing together. You’re listed only with your consent.

  2. 2

    You get the link

    A personal link, on WhatsApp. You open it on your phone or computer, and in the app too. No account, no password.

  3. 3

    You update whenever you like

    A product sold out, a holiday, some news: one tap and the listing changes straight away, on the site and in the app.

What you do from your page

  • Products and prices

    Available or sold out with one tap. Anyone waiting for a product gets a notification when it’s back.

  • Opening hours and closures

    The week, holidays from… to…, and “I’m closed today” for the unexpected.

  • “Hold it for me”

    Customers ask you to hold products for the day they come by. You reply with one tap: I’ll hold it, I don’t have it, or another day.

  • The request calendar

    The month at a glance: how many requests for each day, and which ones are waiting for your reply.

  • News

    “Strawberries from Saturday”: one line, and it shows on your listing and, in the app, to people who follow you.

  • The week’s numbers

    How many people viewed your listing, asked for directions, follow you. Anonymous counts.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Il mio contadino is free, for farms and for people who buy.

Do I need an account or a password?

No. You sign in with your personal link. If you like, you can save your access with your email and then also sign in with a 6-digit code.

Who sees my data?

The listing shows what you decide: name, address, opening hours, products and the farm’s phone number. The WhatsApp number you leave us for the link is only used to get in touch with you.

How do I change the address, photos or phone?

We change them for you: write it on your page, under “The farm”, and we’ll reply on WhatsApp.

What if I don’t have time to update?

When nothing changes, one tap on “Is everything right?” is enough. The listing shows the date of the last check, and customers trust that.

Can I remove the listing?

Yes, whenever you like. Write to us and we’ll hide it.
