Privacy

Who handles the data

Data controller: Muse Pixel, ciao@ilmiocontadino.com. This page says which data the Il mio contadino site and app use, where it is kept and for how long.

The site

The public pages (farms, towns, products, markets) use no cookies, no analytics tools and no scripts from other services. In your browser, in its storage, stay your choice between list and photos and the farms you save as favourites: without an account they stay there and we don’t receive them. With an account (below), the session and which replies from farms you’ve already seen are kept there too. You delete them with the site data in your browser settings.

Location: if you tap “Near you” or “Use your location”, the browser asks you for it. We use it only in the browser, for distances, and we send it to nobody. On a farm’s page, if you have already allowed the site to use it, the browser uses it to show how far you are, without asking again: there too it stays in the browser.

The pages are already built and are hosted on Hostinger, which, like any service that serves web pages, receives the IP address and technical data of whoever opens them. While you look at them the site doesn’t contact our database, except when you open “Hold it for me” (to read the farm’s days that are still free) and when you’re signed in (for your requests and favourites).

Photos come from Supabase (those uploaded for the farms) or from Unsplash (those of the sample farms and the one at the top of the home page): the browser downloads them from there, and those services also receive the IP address.

“Directions” opens Google Maps only if you tap it; from there Google’s rules apply.

Searching for a place: if in the “Where” step you type a town or city that has no farms, the browser asks Komoot’s Photon (photon.komoot.io) for places, which receives the text you typed and the IP address. The place you choose stays in the page address and we don’t receive it.

The map: only if you open the “Map” view, the browser downloads the map pieces (OpenStreetMap data) from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which receives the IP address and the technical data of the request, with no cookies and no account. The fonts and the program that draws the map (MapLibre) are on our own site.

Report a mistake

If you send a report, the browser gets an anonymous sign-in from Supabase, with no name or email, only for sending. Supabase uses the IP address to limit anonymous sign-ins.

We keep the message, the contact if you leave one, the farm, the date and the anonymous identifier of the sign-in. We use the contact only to ask you about that report. If you want us to delete it, write to us.

The account (optional)

Browsing, searching and saving favourites also work without an account. The account is for sending “Hold it for me” requests from the site and for finding requests and favourites on every device where you sign in.

To sign in you write your email: Supabase, which handles sign-ins for us, sends you a 6-digit code that is valid for 10 minutes. No password. Of the account we keep the email, the date you created it and the date of the last sign-in, and what you do with the account: requests and favourites. The email is used only to send you the code and, when a farm replies to one of your requests, a notice with the reply: farms don’t see it and we send you no advertising. It stays as long as you keep the account.

In the browser, as long as you’re signed in, the session (which renews itself) and which replies from farms you’ve already seen are kept, for the dot in the header; “Sign out” removes the session from that browser. While you sign in, only in that browser tab, the email you asked the code for and the request you’re writing are kept, so they aren’t lost if the page reloads.

With the account, favourites are also on the server, linked to the account, so you find them everywhere: at the first sign-in they are merged with those of the browser.

“Hold it for me” from the site: we save the request linked to your account, with the name you introduce yourself with, the products, the day, the note and the farm’s reply. The farm sees the name, never your email. Without a reply the request expires after 12 hours; in “My requests” you see those of the last 60 days. Requests are deleted automatically 60 days after the collection day.

“Delete account”, in “Your account”, immediately deletes the email, the requests (farms see the ones still open as cancelled) and the favourites saved in the account. It can’t be undone.

Suggest a farm

If you suggest a farm to us, the browser gets an anonymous sign-in from Supabase only for sending, as with reports. We keep the name of the farm, the municipality, what it sells, the contact if you leave one, the date and the anonymous identifier of the sign-in. We use the contact only to tell you when the farm is on Il mio contadino. With “Ask for your link”, from the page for producers, we keep the same data and your WhatsApp number, which we use only to call you and send you the personal link. If you look up the farm’s address there, the browser asks Komoot’s Photon (photon.komoot.io), which receives the text searched (or the point tapped on the map) and the IP address; if you choose a place we also keep the address and the point confirmed on the map. If you want us to delete the suggestion, write to us.

Where the data is kept

Farm listings, accounts, requests, favourites, reports, suggestions and the app’s data are kept in the Il mio contadino database on Supabase, in a project in the eu-west-1 region (Ireland). Emails are sent from our domain (codice@ilmiocontadino.com for the code, avvisi@ilmiocontadino.com for farms’ replies) and delivered by Resend, which receives the email address and the text of the message. Some of these services (Resend, Anthropic PBC for the producer’s AI, and Apple and Google for notifications) are based in the United States and may process data outside the European Union too.

How long we keep the data

“Hold it for me” requests are deleted automatically 60 days after the collection day. Anonymous sign-ins (app and browser without an account) idle for 12 months are deleted together with the followed farms and the alerts, except those that still receive notifications. The account with the email stays until you delete it. Reports and suggestions stay as long as they are needed to correct the listings; if you want us to delete them sooner, write to us.

On what basis

We use the data to give you what you ask for: the account, the requests to farms, the notifications you turn on, your farm’s listing (performance of the service). Abuse checks on anonymous sign-ins and anonymous visit counts serve to keep the service working and useful (legitimate interest). Farms appear only with their consent.

The iPhone and Android apps

The apps sign in to Supabase as an anonymous user: no registration, no name, no email. If you want, you can also sign in from the app with your email, as on the site, and “Delete account” is in Profile. Location is used only on the phone, while you use the app, and is never sent.

Searching for a place in the “Where” step, and for the farm’s address, goes through Apple Maps on iPhone and through Komoot’s Photon on Android: they receive the text typed, not who you are.

“Hold it for me”: when you ask a farm to hold products for you we save the request, meaning the name you introduce yourself with, the products, the day, the note and the farm’s reply; the farm sees them. Without a reply the request expires after 12 hours. Requests are deleted automatically 60 days after the collection day.

Notifications: if you turn them on, the server keeps the Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) notification token, the app language and the random identifier of the anonymous user. On the Android apps, notifications go through Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. They are deleted when you turn notifications off or when Apple or Google say the token is no longer valid.

For news from the farms you follow and for “Let me know when it’s back” the server also keeps the farms you follow and the products you’re waiting for, linked to the same random identifier.

On Android, favourites, shopping list and awaited products are on the phone, and come back with Google backup if you use it. iCloud (iPhone): favourites, shopping list and awaited products are on the phone and, if iCloud is on, in your iCloud (Apple’s key-value store), so you find them on another iPhone. In the iCloud keychain the app saves a random key to find your requests again; on the server we keep only its fingerprint.

The app counts anonymously the visits to listings, the directions requested and the products put on the shopping list, added up by week; the list itself stays on the phone. The answers to “Was it as described?” stay with the farm and the day.

For producers

Listings show only business data: name, address of the point of sale, the business’s phone and website, opening hours, products and prices, photos and description. Never private numbers or addresses.

A farm appears only with the producer’s consent, recorded with the date. The contact for the invitation (name and WhatsApp number) stays in the Il mio contadino dashboard and never appears on the site or in the app. Every change to a listing is recorded, with what changed and when.

If the producer saves access to the farm with their email, we keep the email (in Supabase Auth, only to send the sign-in code) linked to the farm: this way they find it again without a link on another phone or in the browser. The management dashboard shows only that access is saved, not the address. After leaving the producer area, the email no longer opens the farm; “Delete account” deletes it.

The farm’s page, which opens with the personal link, keeps in the browser’s storage the anonymous Supabase sign-in and the chosen language, so you don’t have to reconnect every time; there too, no cookies and no scripts from other services. If you turn on browser notifications, the server keeps the notification address that the browser gives you (managed by its push service: Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft) and the language; they are deleted when you turn them off or when the browser says the address is no longer valid.

In the farm area there is a box “Write what has changed”. If the producer uses it, the sentence they write and the list of their products (names, availability, prices) are sent to Anthropic (United States) to work out which changes to propose; we don’t send email, phone, address or other data of the farm. The AI saves nothing: the producer confirms every change. To limit use, we count how many requests each farm makes per day (only the number and the time, not the text).

On request we remove or correct the listing: write to ciao@ilmiocontadino.com.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data about you by writing to ciao@ilmiocontadino.com, and you can contact the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
