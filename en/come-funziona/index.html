How Il mio contadino works

What it is

Il mio contadino lists the farms in South Tyrol that sell directly: at the farm, at the market or from a vending machine. For each farm you can see what it sells, approximate prices, opening hours and how to get there.

You buy on the spot, from the people who make the food. Il mio contadino sells nothing, and there are no online orders or payments.

Who updates the listings

Producers update their own listings from their phone, with a personal link: opening hours, products and prices, closing days and news.

Each listing shows when the producer last updated it. After three months without an update it says “to be checked”. The pages of the site are rebuilt every hour.

“Hold it for me”

On a farm’s page, choose the products with + and ask the farm to hold them for the day you’re coming by. The farm replies with one tap: it will hold them, it doesn’t have them, or it suggests another day. You’ll find the reply in “My requests”. It’s not an order and you pay nothing: you pay at the farm.

An account, if you want one

To browse, search and save favourites you need nothing. With an account you send requests to farms and find your requests and favourites on every device. Your email is enough: we send you a 6-digit code, no password. You can delete the account whenever you like from “Your account”.

It’s free

Il mio contadino is free for people who search and for people who sell. You don’t need to register to search, and the site uses no cookies and shows no ads.

Do you have a farm?

If you sell directly and want to be listed, see the page for producers: we send you a personal link on WhatsApp, no password. You’re listed only with your consent, and you can ask us at any time to correct or remove your listing (contact).

The app

With the iPhone and Android app you can also follow farms and get their news, ask farms to hold products for you with “Hold it for me”, and plan your shopping list. It’s coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.
